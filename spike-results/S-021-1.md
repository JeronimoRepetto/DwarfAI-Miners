# S-021-1 — Claude transcript `stop_reason` as a turn end

## Question

Spike register S-021-1 (ADR-021 item 4): for a Claude session DwarfAI only observes through its transcript, is a
terminal `stop_reason` on the last assistant record a reliable end of the turn? The cases to cover: a tool-use
continuation (the turn goes on after `tool_use`), compaction, and subagents.

## Setup

The evidence is a set of real-CLI recordings with the maintainer's own login (`17` §5.5). This issue built:

- `spikes/S-021-1/claudeTurnEnds.mjs`: per main-chain turn (a turn starts at a prompt that is not a tool result or a
  summary), the assistant messages (one per `message.id`, with the stop reason of its last row that has one), the tool
  uses, how many messages ended with a terminal stop reason (`end_turn`, `stop_sequence`, `max_tokens`, `refusal`),
  and the markers of the listed cases (a `compact_boundary` record, side-chain records of a subagent). A turn has one
  reliable end when exactly one message ended it and it is the turn's last; the transcript is `reliable` when every
  turn has. Subagent (side-chain) records never count as the turn's end. Counts and stop reasons only.
- The cases, in `spikes/SP-06/observer-cases.md`: `one-turn`, `tool-continuation`, `two-turns`, `subagent`,
  `compaction`; captured with `spikes/SP-06/captureTranscript.mjs` and scrubbed with `scripts/fixtures/scrub.mjs`.
- `spikes/S-021-1/claudeTurnEnds.os.test.mjs` (OS lane, synthetic input): a tool-use continuation with a partial row,
  a compaction with a subagent's own end, a turn ending on a tool use, and a turn with two ends.

## Decision

- **`turnEnd` stays `none` for "Claude observed, transcript only" (ADR-021 item 4). Its ends are emitted with
  `reliability: 'inferred'`**: they may move the dwarf to `idle` (ADR-032 item 5), and they never fire the finished
  cue, the level-3 notification or "Turn finished" wording. ISSUE-100 builds that, and its test
  `[S-021-1] a Claude transcript-only end stays inferred while the capability record says turnEnd none` holds it.
- **Passed for a Claude version** means: `claudeTurnEnds.mjs` reports `reliable: true` on the scrubbed recordings of
  all five cases of that version, and those fixtures are committed under `fixtures/claude/observer/<version>/`, where
  the C-20 observer cases read them. Then `15` §3 records `turnEnd: 'reliable'` for that channel with version and date
  (NFR-OBS-04), and ISSUE-100 emits `reliable` ends from it.
